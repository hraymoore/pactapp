## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Pact is the contract platform built for how modern business actually gets agreed to.

DRAFT FROM A TEMPLATE
Start from an attorney-informed template across 20+ business genres — freelance, real estate, technology, construction, and more — or upload your own document. No blank page, ever.

NEGOTIATE WITH REAL EDITS
A full in-line editor lets both sides work the actual contract text, not a PDF mark-up. Every change is tracked.

SIGN WITH SECURE E-SIGNATURE
Send a contract for signature through secure, compliant e-signature infrastructure. Signers don't need a Pact account to review and sign what's sent to them.

NOTHING CHANGES QUIETLY
Once both parties sign, Pact locks the executed version and time-stamps every edit that follows — so there's always a clear record of who changed what, and when.

OPTIONAL AI ASSISTANCE
On Professional and Enterprise tiers, Pact AI drafts a first pass from a plain-language prompt, suggests clause-level alternatives as you negotiate, and can summarize or flag risk in a contract before you sign. Pact AI assists with drafting and review — it does not provide legal advice.

BUILT FOR TEAMS
Enterprise accounts get a shared Business Directory — unlimited team members, role-based permissions, and a shared contract pool — plus optional two-factor authentication for admins.

FREE TO START
Create a profile, browse the full template library, and sign anything sent to you — free, no card required. Upgrade any time to send contracts yourself, unlock the editor, and turn on Pact AI.

Visit pactappstore.com to learn more.

## Data safety form — what to disclose
Based on what Pact actually collects (server/src/db.js, website/privacy.html):
- **Personal info collected**: name, email address, and (personal accounts) legal name + date of birth for legal-capacity verification — not shown to other users, only your display name is.
- **Financial info**: handled entirely by Stripe's hosted Checkout — Pact never receives or stores card numbers.
- **Identity documents**: only if a user opts into Stripe Identity verification — the document/selfie/SSN goes directly to Stripe, Pact stores only a pass/fail result and the verified name.
- **App activity**: in-app actions (contract views/edits/signatures) are logged for the audit trail feature — this is core product functionality, not analytics/tracking.
- **Data sharing**: shared with Stripe (payments, identity verification), Anthropic (only when a paid-tier user actively uses Pact AI), and SendGrid (transactional email delivery only) — see the live Security page (pactappstore.com/security.html) for the current list.
- **Data deletion**: users can close their own account from Settings (soft-delete — see Privacy Policy §6) and self-serve export their own data via the "Download My Data" button in Settings.
- **Encryption in transit**: yes (HTTPS).
- No advertising, no third-party analytics/tracking SDKs, no data sold.
