About Pact

Contracts shouldn't live in your inbox.

Pact was built on a simple observation: agreements are the backbone of every business relationship, yet most people still manage them across scattered PDFs, email chains and sticky notes. We built the platform we wished existed — one home for creating, negotiating, signing and tracking every contract you're party to.

Why we built this

Whether you're a freelancer sending your first services agreement, a landlord managing a dozen leases, or an enterprise legal team pushing a master agreement through redlines — the process looks the same everywhere: draft, negotiate, sign, and then hope everyone remembers what changed after the fact.

Pact is the first platform designed around that entire lifecycle, not just the signature at the end. We treat the moment of signing as a milestone, not a finish line — every edit made afterward is captured, time-stamped, and attributed to the person who made it.

What we believe

  • ✓ Contracts should be written in language people actually understand.
  • ✓ Every agreement needs an owner — which is why Pact is profile-first.
  • ✓ Trust is built on a record, not a memory. Time stamps aren't optional.
  • ✓ AI should make contracts safer to sign, not harder to understand.
  • ✓ Security and simplicity are not a trade-off.
Our approach

Built profile-first, template-rich, and audit-native

👤

Profile-first by design

Nothing in Pact — viewing, editing, downloading or sending a contract — happens without a verified profile. Anonymous access stops at the landing page.

📚

A template for every genre

From freelance services to enterprise franchise agreements, our library spans the business categories real people actually contract in.

🕓

Change history that can't be quiet

Once two parties sign, Pact locks the executed version and time-stamps every edit that follows — so nothing changes without a record.

Where we're headed

Web today. iOS, Android and your desk tomorrow.

Pact is launching as a web platform first, with native mobile apps for iOS and Android in active development so you can review and sign contracts wherever you are. We're also building secure integrations for e-signature and identity verification, so the trust infrastructure behind every contract meets the same bar as the platform itself.
