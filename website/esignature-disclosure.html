Pact — Electronic Signature Disclosure & Consent

Version 1.0 · Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Drafted with AI assistance and not reviewed by a licensed attorney. This is not legal advice. This disclosure supplements Pact's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and does not replace them.

1. Your Consent to Electronic Records and Signatures

Before you sign or receive a contract electronically through Pact, federal law (the E-SIGN Act) and applicable state law (the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act) require us to tell you the following and get your consent.

By checking the box to accept this disclosure, you agree that:

  • You consent to conduct this transaction, and future transactions with Pact, electronically.
  • Your electronic signature on a contract has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature.
  • You have the hardware and software described in Section 4 below needed to access and retain electronic records.

2. Your Right to Paper Copies

You have the right to receive a paper copy of any contract you sign through Pact. To request one, email legal@pactappstore.com with the contract name and the parties involved; we will provide a paper copy within a reasonable time. We do not charge a fee for this.

3. How to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent to receive records electronically at any time by emailing legal@pactappstore.com. If you withdraw consent, this may affect your ability to use e-signature features going forward, and any contracts already signed electronically remain valid and binding — withdrawal is not retroactive.

4. Hardware & Software Requirements

To access, view, and retain records through Pact, you need: a device with an up-to-date web browser, an active internet connection, a valid email address, and the ability to save or print a PDF document. If these requirements change in a way that creates a material risk you can no longer access your records, we will notify you and ask for renewed consent.

5. Updating Your Contact Information

You must keep your email address on file with Pact current so you can receive notices related to your electronic records. Update it any time from Account Settings.

6. Scope

This disclosure applies to every contract you send, sign, view, or receive through the Service. It supplements, and does not replace, Pact's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this disclosure: legal@pactappstore.com
