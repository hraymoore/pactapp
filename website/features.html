Access & identity

Nothing happens without a profile

👤

Profile-gated access

Viewing, editing, downloading and sending a contract all require a verified Pact profile. The public site is a landing page only.

🔐

Encrypted storage

Contract content is encrypted at rest and in transit, with per-profile access controls and scoped sharing to named counterparties.

🧾

Role-based permissions

Everyday and above let you invite teammates with view, edit or admin-level access, so control stays with account owners.

📱

Two-factor authentication

Optional two-factor authentication for every profile, required for business admins before they can invite teammates or change roles.

e-Signature

Signing built on trusted, compliant infrastructure

Pact's editor is where contracts get written and negotiated — signing is handled through secure, compliant e-signature infrastructure designed to meet ESIGN Act and UETA standards in the U.S., and eIDAS where applicable. We're building Pact to interoperate with established, secure signing providers such as Adobe Acrobat Sign and DocuSign, so your signature workflow meets the same bar as the rest of your business software.

Audit trail

Once two parties sign, the clock starts

Every contract has two lives: the negotiation before signing, and the record after. Pact locks the executed version the moment both parties sign, then time-stamps every subsequent edit — who made it, exactly when, and what changed.
