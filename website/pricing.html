Pricing FAQ

Can I change tiers later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from your profile settings. Changes apply to your next billing cycle.

What happens to contracts if I downgrade?

All existing signed contracts and their time-stamped history remain fully accessible, even on Starter.

Is e-signature legally binding?

Pact routes signatures through secure, compliant e-signature infrastructure. See our Security & AI page for details.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, once you create a profile you can switch to annual billing for roughly 15% savings on any tier.
