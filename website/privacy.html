Pact — Privacy Policy

Draft v0.2 · Last Updated: September 1, 2026

DRAFT — REQUIRES ATTORNEY REVIEW BEFORE PUBLICATION. This describes what Pact App Store's software actually collects and does with it today, written directly from the codebase rather than generic boilerplate — but it has not been reviewed against the California Consumer Privacy Act, the General Data Protection Regulation, other state privacy statutes, or any other applicable law, and Pact App Store has not yet finalized its transactional-email provider (Section 4) or how data-subject requests (access, deletion, correction) get fulfilled in practice (Sections 6 and 7). Do not publish this as binding until an attorney has reviewed it and those two items are settled.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Pact collects when you use the Service, why, who else sees it, and what choices you have. It's incorporated by reference into Pact's Terms of Service; terms defined there have the same meaning here.

Pact does not use third-party advertising trackers, ad pixels, or behavioral analytics scripts — there is nothing like that in the site today.

3. Cookies

Pact sets one cookie: a session token (pact_token) that keeps you logged in. It's marked httpOnly (not readable by page scripts) and, in production, secure (HTTPS-only). We don't use third-party or advertising cookies.

4. Who We Share Information With

Pact shares information only with the service providers ("sub-processors") needed to run the features you use, and with the other parties on a contract you send or are added to. We do not sell personal information.

  • Stripe — payment processing, subscription billing, and (if you use it) identity verification. Stripe receives and stores your payment details and, for identity verification, your government-issued identification, photograph, or Social Security Number directly — Pact never receives or stores that data itself, only Stripe's verification result.
  • Anthropic — if you're on a tier with Pact AI and you use it, your prompt (and the contract text you're asking about, if any) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use an AI feature.
  • Email delivery — transactional email only (password resets, signing-link notifications, attorney-review confirmations), sent via [SMTP/EMAIL PROVIDER NAME — not yet finalized]. No marketing email is sent.
  • Other users you designate — a contract's counterparty, a share recipient, or a fellow member of your Business Directory can see the contract(s) you've given them access to, and your name/email as it appears on that contract.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Pact or others.

5. Identity Verification — What We Deliberately Don't Store

If you complete identity verification, Pact hands you off to Stripe Identity's own hosted, compliant verification flow. Stripe captures and stores the sensitive document image, photograph, or Social Security Number data — Pact's own database stores only the verification result (pass/fail) and your verified name, never the raw document or Social Security Number itself. This is a deliberate design choice: that data carries compliance obligations (breach-notice laws, state biometric-privacy statutes) that make sense for a dedicated identity-verification company to carry, not a platform like Pact.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and contract data for as long as your account is active, since a contract's whole value is being retrievable later — including after a contract is fully signed. Terms-of-Service acceptance records (Section 8) are kept permanently as an append-only audit trail and are never deleted, even if you close your account, since they're the evidence that an agreement was formed. [Retention period after account closure for other data, and a self-serve export/delete flow, are not yet built or finalized — flag for attorney and product review before publishing.]

7. Your Choices

You can update your name, password, and (where applicable) business details from Settings at any time. For anything else — including a request to access, correct, or delete your personal information — contact privacy@pactappstore.com; Pact does not yet have a self-serve data export or account-deletion tool, so these requests are handled manually today.

8. Terms-of-Service Acceptance Records

When you check the box to accept Pact's Terms of Service (at signup, and again if the Terms materially change), we record which version you accepted, when, the IP address you accepted from, and how (e.g., the signup checkbox). This is kept as permanent evidence that the agreement was formed and is never edited or deleted.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text). Session cookies are httpOnly and, in production, HTTPS-only. Payment details never touch Pact's servers — they go directly to Stripe. That said, no system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the Service.

10. Children's Privacy

Pact is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, anyone under 18 — consistent with the Terms of Service's eligibility requirement (Section 2).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated the same way Terms updates are — see the Terms of Service, Section 19.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request regarding your data: privacy@pactappstore.com
