Pact — Privacy Policy

Draft v0.3 · Last Updated: September 1, 2026

DRAFT — REQUIRES ATTORNEY REVIEW BEFORE PUBLICATION. This describes what Pact App Store's software actually collects and does with it today, written directly from the codebase rather than generic boilerplate — but it has not been reviewed against the California Consumer Privacy Act, the General Data Protection Regulation, other state privacy statutes, or any other applicable law, and Pact App Store has not yet finalized its transactional-email provider (Section 4). Do not publish this as binding until an attorney has reviewed it and that item is settled.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Pact collects when you use the Service, why, who else sees it, and what choices you have. It's incorporated by reference into Pact's Terms of Service; terms defined there have the same meaning here.

3. Cookies

Pact sets one cookie: a session token (pact_token) that keeps you logged in. It's marked httpOnly (not readable by page scripts) and, in production, secure (HTTPS-only). We don't use third-party or advertising cookies.

4. Who We Share Information With

Pact shares information only with the service providers ("sub-processors") needed to run the features you use, and with the other parties on a contract you send or are added to. We do not sell personal information.

  • Stripe — payment processing, subscription billing, and (if you use it) identity verification. Stripe receives and stores your payment details and, for identity verification, your government-issued identification, photograph, or Social Security Number directly — Pact never receives or stores that data itself, only Stripe's verification result.
  • Anthropic — if you're on a tier with Pact AI and you use it, your prompt (and the contract text you're asking about, if any) is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response. This only happens when you actively use an AI feature.
  • Email delivery — transactional email only (password resets, signing-link notifications, attorney-review confirmations), sent via [SMTP/EMAIL PROVIDER NAME — not yet finalized]. No marketing email is sent.
  • Other users you designate — a contract's counterparty, a share recipient, or a fellow member of your Business Directory can see the contract(s) you've given them access to, and your name/email as it appears on that contract.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Pact or others.

5. Identity Verification — What We Deliberately Don't Store

If you complete identity verification, Pact hands you off to Stripe Identity's own hosted, compliant verification flow. Stripe captures and stores the sensitive document image, photograph, or Social Security Number data — Pact's own database stores only the verification result (pass/fail) and your verified name, never the raw document or Social Security Number itself. This is a deliberate design choice: that data carries compliance obligations (breach-notice laws, state biometric-privacy statutes) that make sense for a dedicated identity-verification company to carry, not a platform like Pact.

6. Account Closure & Data Retention

You can close your account at any time from Settings. Closing it immediately disables login, cancels any recurring subscription, and removes your profile from search, sharing, and invite lookups — but it does not immediately erase your data. Here's exactly what happens, and when:

Contracts are not solely yours to delete. Because a contract may involve one or more other parties, closing your account does not delete contracts to which another party is also a signatory or named counterparty — their access to a shared, executed contract is preserved regardless of what you do to your own account. We retain executed contracts and their full audit trail (creation, edit, view, and signature history) for seven (7) years following the contract's execution or termination date, or longer if required by law, an open dispute, or a legal hold, whichever is later.

Personal profile data not required to preserve an executed contract or satisfy a legal retention obligation — your name, contact information, and profile details not embedded in a signed contract — will be deleted or de-identified within thirty (30) days of account closure, provided no contract tied to your profile is active, pending signature, or subject to dispute or legal hold. Terms-of-Service acceptance records (Section 8) are the one exception kept permanently, even after closure, since they're the evidence an agreement was formed.

Manual review. Some closure or deletion requests are routed for manual review rather than processed automatically — including where a contract tied to the account is actively in dispute, mid-signature, under a fraud or security investigation, or subject to a legal hold. You'll be notified if that applies to you and given an expected timeframe.

Pact keeps an internal record of each account closure and deletion request — what was deleted, what was retained, and why — for our own compliance and audit purposes.

7. Your Choices

You can update your name, password, and (where applicable) business details from Settings at any time, and close your account yourself from the same page (see Section 6). Where applicable law — such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or comparable state privacy laws — gives you the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information beyond what account closure already covers, submit that request to privacy@pactappstore.com; we will respond within the time required by applicable law. We may deny or limit a request to the extent retention is necessary to complete a transaction, detect security incidents, comply with a legal obligation, or as otherwise permitted by law, including the contract-retention period described in Section 6. Pact does not yet have a fully automated self-serve data-export tool — an export request today is fulfilled manually.

8. Terms-of-Service Acceptance Records

When you check the box to accept Pact's Terms of Service (at signup, and again if the Terms materially change), we record which version you accepted, when, the IP address you accepted from, and how (e.g., the signup checkbox). This is kept as permanent evidence that the agreement was formed and is never edited or deleted.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text). Session cookies are httpOnly and, in production, HTTPS-only. Payment details never touch Pact's servers — they go directly to Stripe. That said, no system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the Service.

10. Children's Privacy

Pact is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, anyone under 18 — consistent with the Terms of Service's eligibility requirement (Section 2).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated the same way Terms updates are — see the Terms of Service, Section 19.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request regarding your data: privacy@pactappstore.com
